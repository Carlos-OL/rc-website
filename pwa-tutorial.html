Save the App to Your Home Screen

Install the website as a PWA (Progressive Web App) to receive push notifications on your phone — even when your browser isn't open.

First — log in on your mobile browser
①
On your phone, go to restockbotalerts.com and tap Customer Portal at the top of the page
→
②
Enter your email to log in — you'll receive a magic link to your inbox
→
③
Tap the magic link on your phone — it will open the dashboard in a new browser tab. Now save it from there.
🍎
iPhone / iPad
Safari or Chrome
  1. 1
    With the dashboard open in your browser, tap the Share button
    It's the box-with-arrow icon at the bottom of the screen (or top on iPad).
  2. 2
    Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
    Give it a name if you'd like, then tap Add in the top right.
  3. 3
    Open the app from your home screen icon
    Important: always launch from the icon — not from the browser.
  4. 4
    The app asks you to turn on alerts — tap Turn on alerts, then Allow
    A test alert arrives on this phone right away, and from then on alerts arrive like native app notifications. Tapped Not now? Turn alerts on any time in Account → Settings (on a phone, tap the ☰ menu button at the top right first).
    Tapped “Don't Allow”? Here's the fix ↓
🤖
Android
Chrome browser required
  1. 1
    With the dashboard open in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu in the top right
    This is the three-dot menu at the top right of the Chrome browser.
  2. 2
    Tap Add to Home Screen or Install App
    The exact label may vary slightly by Android version — both do the same thing.
  3. 3
    Tap Install to confirm
    The app icon will appear on your home screen immediately.
  4. 4
    Open the app from the icon — it asks you to turn on alerts. Tap Turn on alerts, then Allow
    Always launch from the home screen icon — not from Chrome. A test alert arrives right away. Tapped Not now? Turn alerts on any time in Account → Settings (on a phone, tap the ☰ menu button at the top right first).
    Tapped “Don't Allow”? Here's the fix ↓
⚠️
Important — Enable Notifications From the App, Not the Browser
Once the icon is on your home screen, always open the site from that icon — not from Safari or Chrome. Push notifications are only active when you're running the PWA. After opening it from the icon, tap Turn on alerts when the app asks (or go to Account → Settings → Push Notifications) and choose the alerts you want. This step must be done from within the PWA.
Alerts blocked?

Tapped “Don't Allow”?

The app can only ask once. If you tapped Don't Allow, it can't ask again — turn alerts back on in your phone's settings:

🍎
iPhone / iPad
  1. Open your phone's Settings app
  2. Tap Notifications, then Gold Bot (or the name the app has on your home screen)
  3. Turn on Allow Notifications
🤖
Android
  1. Press and hold the Gold Bot app icon, then tap App info
  2. Tap Notifications and turn them on

Or in Chrome: tap ⋮ → Settings → Site settings → Notifications, and allow dashboard.restockbotalerts.com.

Then come back to the app (open it from the icon) and tap Turn on alerts, or go to Account → Settings → Push Notifications.

Which alerts you get

Alerts work without Slack

You don't need Slack — these alerts come straight to your phone through the app. Turn each one on or off in Account → Settings → Push Notifications; the name in brackets is the switch you'll see there. Follow products and set up payout watch lists on the dashboard's Watchlists page — no Slack needed. In-store alerts have no switch: they follow your saved in-store search.

  • All online restocks (All Online Restocks)
    An alert for every online restock at Costco.
  • Only the products you follow (Subscribed SKUs Only)
    A filter on restock alerts: follow products on the Watchlists page (Online Restocks tab), turn this on, and restock alerts come only for those products.
  • Gold & silver price alerts (Spot Price Alerts)
    When gold or silver hits your price targets.
  • Payout alerts (Payout Alerts)
    When a product hits your payout target. Set up your payout watch list on the Watchlists page (Payouts tab).
  • In-store stock alerts (no switch)
    When your saved in-store stock search finds the product at a nearby warehouse. These follow your saved in-store search.
  • Price error alerts (Price Error Alerts)
    When someone at Costco fat-fingers a price and an item is listed at a mistaken price. Gold plan only.
Push notification settings are per device — repeat these steps on each device where you want alerts.
All other settings (cashback %, Pure tier, buyer visibility) sync automatically across all your devices.
